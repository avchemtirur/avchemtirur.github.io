25. Functional Requirements (FR) 9
FR-001 Login

OTP‑based login (Mobile). Google/Social option. Session timeout 15 min.

FR-002 QR Scan

Camera scan + Torch On/Off + Gallery pick. Shows Coupon details (Name, Expiry, Status).

FR-003 Coupon Verification

Validity check (Expiry, Status, Dealer). Valid → "Activate/Verify" button enabled.

FR-004 Reward Redemption

Show total Points. Min 100 pts to redeem. Deduct points & log transaction.

FR-005 Customer Registration

Register with Mobile, Name, Email. First‑time bonus: 10 Welcome Points.

FR-006 Dashboard

Role‑based summary cards: Total Scans, Active Coupons, Customers, Points Redeemed.

FR-007 Dealer & Staff Mgmt

Super Admin manages Dealers. Dealer manages own Staff (CRUD).

FR-008 Transaction History

Detailed log with filters: Timestamp, User, Coupon Code, Status.

FR-009 Push Notifications

In‑app + Firebase for Verified, Points Credited, Redeemed, Expiry alerts.

28. Business Rules 7
🔁 Unique Redemption

One coupon code can be verified only once per customer. Duplicate → ERR002

⏳ Points Expiry

Points validity = 1 year. Expired points auto‑reset.

📊 Minimum Threshold

Customer needs ≥ 100 Points to redeem a reward.

🚫 Cancelled Coupon

Cancelled coupons cannot be scanned/verified. Shows ERR001

📅 Daily Scan Limit

Each Staff can scan max 500 coupons per day (anti‑abuse).

🎁 New User Bonus

First‑time registration → 10 Bonus Points (one‑time).

↩️ Reverse Points

Product return → points deducted (negative points log).
