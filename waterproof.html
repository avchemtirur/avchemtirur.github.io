Application Areas For Waterproofing In Construction


Foundation

Waterproofing the foundation of your home and building prevents the structure from becoming weak, making your home more durable.

Roofs

Applying waterproof chemicals to the roof of your homes and buildings will protect its mainframe from rain, snow and frost.

Walls

By constructing your walls to be moisture and dampness resistant with waterproofing solutions, the concrete foundations are rendered waterproof.

Balconies

Balconies require waterproofing materials because they are one of the first areas to come into contact with water during rains.

Terrace

The terrace of your homes and buildings on being exposed to incessant rains can become damp and damaged without waterproofing.

Basement

Moisture seeping into your basement can cause metals to rust and wood to decay. Using waterproofing material prevents this.

Water tanks

Proper waterproofing for water tanks ensures hygiene and structural integrity preventing leakages.
